What is Roomie Connect?
Roomie Connect is a smart roommate-finding platform that matches people based on lifestyle — food habits, personality, sleep schedule and budget. Whether you're a student, working professional, or relocating to a new city, we help you find the right person to share your space with.
🎯
Our Mission
Finding a roommate shouldn't be random. We believe compatibility goes beyond just location — it's about daily habits, lifestyle, and comfort. Our mission is to make shared living easier, safer and more enjoyable for everyone — students, professionals, and anyone in between.

How It Works
  • 1
    Create an account and fill your preferences — diet, schedule, personality, budget and city.
  • 2
    Browse profiles filtered by your lifestyle. See a compatibility score for each person.
  • 3
    Send a connection request with a personal message to anyone you like.
  • 4
    Once they accept, chat in real-time and plan your accommodation together.
  • 5
    Use the Hostel & PG Map to find accommodation near your preferred location.
